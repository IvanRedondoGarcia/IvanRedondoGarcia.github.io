Support

Quick answers to the questions we get most. Can’t find yours? Write to us.

How do I save my progress?

Your progress is saved automatically on your phone and in the cloud. To never lose it, connect your Google account in Settings → Game. If you play as a guest, your progress is lost when you delete the game.

How do I move my progress to another phone?

Connect the same Google account on both devices (Settings → Game). Signing in with that account loads your progress.

I lost my progress

Write to contact@lancegamesdv.com with your player ID (Settings → Game → Player ID) and anything you remember about your game (name, level). We will look into it.

I bought something and didn’t get it

Open the Shop and tap “Restore purchases”. If it still hasn’t arrived, write to contact@lancegamesdv.com with your Google Play receipt.

I found a bug

In the game: Settings → Info → “Report a problem”. You can also tell us in the bugs channel of our Discord.

Is the game free?

Yes. Purchases are optional and everything can be earned by playing. Odds for random rewards are shown in the game.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the game: Settings → Game → “Delete game”. You can also ask by email; we explain it step by step here. How to delete your account
