About the game

A funny, epic idle RPG: a vampire-hunting garlic against Count Vampire and his army of bats, zombies and werewolves.

A vampire-hunting garlic leaves its magic garden and travels through legendary lands to the castle of Count Vampire. Pull garlics to get gear, evolve your hero, collect pets and mounts, and compete with players worldwide in clans, the arena and leaderboards.

Features

  • 15 legendary zones in an endless loop, each with its own boss and tricks
  • Gear in 15 rarities pulled from the magic Garden, with forging up to level 999
  • 3 classes with 5 evolution forms each
  • 50 pets, each from a family with its own effect, and 50 skills
  • Mounts, relics, the Count’s Tower, dungeons and the Magic Farm
  • Clans, Arena, friends, chat and per-server leaderboards
  • Seasonal events and a free season pass
